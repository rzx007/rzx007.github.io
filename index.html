Five principles.

Held to on every project, without exception.

  1. 01

    Read the material first.

    Before a single layout I spend time inside whatever the project is actually made of: the archive, the instrument, the catalogue, the codebase.

  2. 02

    Systems before screens.

    Type scale, spacing, colour and motion decided once, written down, and then used without exception. Screens are the output, not the process.

  3. 03

    Motion is a measurement.

    If an animation isn't telling you where you are, how fast you got there, or what just changed, it's decoration. I keep the ones that measure.

  4. 04

    Type is the interface.

    Most products are read long before they're clicked. Treating the text as the primary control is usually the difference between clear and merely pretty.

  5. 05

    Ship the whole thing.

    Design that stops at a handoff leaves half the work undone. I write the shaders, tune the easing and push the build myself.
